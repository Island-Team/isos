## EOS-owned MuJoCo boundary (2026-10-30)

The current graph puts `eos.process.backend@v1.0` at `cores/eos/layers/process/groups/backend/nodes/simulator`. IsOS still verifies the pinned package, scene, assets, library, socket, and device IDs at validate, apply, and run. At launch it passes `--backend-node`, `--backend-executable`, and `--backend-scene` to `aether-runtime` and propagates the resolved library environment to that child. EOS owns the simulator child and waits for its socket. IsOS directly manages only `eos`, `bridge`, and `config`. The bridge and graph catalog default to loopback ports 16020 and 16021.
